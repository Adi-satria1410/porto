## Brand & Style

This design system blends 1970s bubble pop aesthetics with contemporary neo-brutalism. It is crafted for creative general portfolios, agency showcases, and characterful digital products that prioritize distinctiveness, optimism, and tactile playfulness over sterile corporate minimalism.

### Aesthetic Pillars
- **Curvy Meets Rigid:** Chunky, bubble-inspired display forms juxtaposed with disciplined, monospaced metadata and crisp structural grids.
- **Graphic Impact:** Heavy 3px to 4px black outlines (`#18181B`), hard offset drop shadows with zero blur, and sticker-like badges that feel screenprinted or stamped onto the page.
- **Nostalgic Whimsy:** Warm cream paper textures, cheerful smiling mascot accents, retro sparkle stars (4-point bursts), and candy-coated pastel fills.
- **Tactile Neo-Brutalist Interactivity:** Buttons and cards that physically "press down" on hover and click by shifting translation coordinates into their offset shadows.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in a soft, vintage cream base reminiscent of newsprint and risograph stock, accented by punchy pop tones:

- **Primary (`#FF5E97` - Bubblegum Punch):** High-energy retro pink for primary actions, pill badges, and active focus points.
- **Secondary (`#FFAA2B` - Warm Mustard Gold):** Radiant vintage warm yellow/amber for callout badges, interactive highlights, and secondary buttons.
- **Tertiary (`#45C4B0` - Electric Mint / Cyan):** Refreshing retro mint for balance, ticker strips, tags, and tertiary interactive states.
- **Accent Lilac (`#B8A4FF` - Electric Lavender):** Complementary pop tone for decorative stickers, hovered tags, and auxiliary cards.
- **Accent Coral (`#FF8B66` - Coral Peach):** Warm contrast tone for alert accents and illustrative shapes.
- **Neutral Base (`#F6E7CA` - Antique Cream):** Canvas base surface. Warmer than stark white, evoking retro print ephemera.
- **Ink Black (`#18181B`):** Deep carbon black reserved for heavy 3–4px border strokes, structural rules, and hard drop shadows.

## Typography

The type system is anchored in a deliberate contrast between bubbly, expressive 70s display letterforms and technical, typewriter-inspired monospace text:

- **Display & Headlines (`Bricolage Grotesque`):** Set at heavy weights (`700` and `800`) with tight letter-spacing. Headlines evoke retro comic book covers, festival signage, and funky poster design.
- **Body, Navigation, & Metadata (`Space Mono`):** Clean, monospaced typewriter structure that provides neo-brutalist grounding, technical legibility, and high utility for portfolio metadata (dates, project tags, tech stacks, client credits).
- **Text Transformations:** Ticker text, utility pills, and section markers are styled with uppercase styling and slight tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.04em`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a heavy-framed, neo-brutalist column grid encased within visible structural borders.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Framed Canvas:** The primary viewport is encased in an outer perimeter frame with a `3px` solid `#18181B` border.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid, `2.5rem` outer margin, `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid, `2rem` outer margin, `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column grid, `1.25rem` outer margin, `1rem` gutters. Cards and multi-column metadata collapse into stacked rows.

### Modular Rhythm
- Components leverage distinct block sections separated by full-width `3px` or `4px` black horizontal division borders.
- Header bars, marquee tickers, and footer blocks stretch border-to-border, creating tactile compartments across the screen.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids blurred Gaussian drop shadows and translucent glass blurs in favor of flat, hard-edged **pop-art brutalist elevation**:

### Shadow Language
- **Default Hard Shadow:** `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #18181B`. Used on buttons, cards, and input modules.
- **Prominent / Hero Shadow:** `box-shadow: 6px 6px 0px #18181B` or layered double-offset: `4px 4px 0px #FFFFFF, 8px 8px 0px #18181B`.
- **Badge / Sticker Shadow:** `box-shadow: 3px 3px 0px #18181B`.

### Stroke Standard
- All interactive controls, cards, modals, and stickers must feature a crisp `3px` or `4px` solid border (`#18181B`).

### Interactive Feedback ("The Depress Effect")
- **Hover:** The element shifts slightly diagonally: `transform: translate(-2px, -2px)` with shadow expanding to `6px 6px 0px #18181B`.
- **Active / Pressed:** The element shifts into the shadow base: `transform: translate(4px, 4px)` with shadow collapsing to `0px 0px 0px #18181B`.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary combines rounded neo-brutalist contours with organic, sticker-like cuts:

- **Standard Containers & Cards (`roundedness: 2`):** `0.5rem` (`8px`) border radius, finished with thick `3px` black borders.
- **Buttons & Pills:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to reinforce the 1970s playful, bubbly feel.
- **Mac OS / Window Emulators:** White modal boxes featuring top rounded corners with three decorative black-bordered window dots (`#FF5E97`, `#FFAA2B`, `#45C4B0`).
- **Starburst Badges & Stickers:** Starbursts, scalloped flower shapes, and 4-point sparkle stars with 3px solid black outlines placed at slight rotation angles (`-3deg` to `+5deg`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `#FF5E97` (Bubblegum) fill, `3px` solid `#18181B` border, `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #18181B`. Font: `Space Mono` Bold, `15px`.
- **Secondary Button:** Same geometry with `#FFAA2B` (Mustard) or `#45C4B0` (Mint) fill.
- **Interaction:** On hover, translates `translate(-2px, -2px)` with `6px 6px 0px` shadow; on click/active, translates `translate(4px, 4px)` with zero shadow.

### Cards & Work Tiles
- Crisp `#FFFFFF` or `#F6E7CA` background, `3px` solid `#18181B` border, `0.75rem` radius, with `6px 6px 0px #18181B` hard offset shadow.
- Optional header ribbon or simulated classic window title bar with colored dot glyphs.

### Chips & Badges
- **Pill Badges:** Small pill contours (`rounded-full`), padded `0.25rem 0.75rem`, `2px` black border, `2px 2px 0px #18181B` shadow, filled with `#FFAA2B`, `#45C4B0`, or `#B8A4FF`.
- **Sticker Badges:** Scalloped flower petals or 12-point jagged starbursts with tilted orientation (`transform: rotate(-6deg)`), containing bold, punchy display words (e.g., "FEATURED", "NEW", "LIVE").

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Boxy pill or `8px` rounded rectangles with `#FFFFFF` background, `3px` solid `#18181B` border, and `4px 4px 0px #18181B` shadow.
- Placeholder text set in `Space Mono` regular with `#71717A`.
- On focus, border remains `3px solid #18181B` and card glow is replaced with an intensified offset shadow in `#FF5E97` or `#45C4B0`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkbox:** Square `22px x 22px`, `3px` solid `#18181B` border, `3px 3px 0px #18181B` hard shadow. Checked state fills with `#45C4B0` and a bold black checkmark.
- **Radio:** Circle `22px x 22px`, `3px` solid `#18181B` border. Selected state shows a solid black or `#FF5E97` inner disc with `3px` inset gap.

### Ticker Strips / Marquees
- Full-bleed black horizontal bars (`#18181B`) with `3px` top and bottom borders.
- Content: `Space Mono` Bold, uppercase, colored in `#45C4B0` (Mint) or `#FFFFFF`, separated by prominent slashes (`/`) or sparkle glyphs (`✦`).
